01 / Personal project · Runs locally

SnapGrade is an AI-powered study application that transforms notes into interactive quizzes and flashcards. With multiple question formats, answer explanations, mistake review, and progress tracking, it helps students test their understanding, identify learning gaps, and build confidence through practice.

ReactTailwind CSSViteNode.jsSupabaseGroq

Inside SnapGrade / Interactive walkthrough

Reading is the start.
Recall is the test.

Follow a short lesson from notes to questions, then see what you understood and what needs another look.

The purpose
Turn study material into opportunities to practice, with explanations that help connect an answer back to the notes.
My contribution
Creator and developer of SnapGrade, working with React, Tailwind CSS, Vite, Node.js, Supabase, and Groq.
Why this walkthrough
Choosing an answer, getting feedback, and reviewing mistakes shows the study flow more clearly than a screenshot alone.

This portfolio sample uses preset questions. It demonstrates the flow without calling the live app or generating questions with AI.
